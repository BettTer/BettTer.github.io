## 中文

今天把个人网站重新收拾了一遍。

想给自己留一间黄昏时分的海边工作室：窗外有粉色的天空、海面和远处的城市，桌上放着一台 CD 机。每换一张碟，就翻到一个做过的项目。写过的东西则叠成几份报纸，放在旁边，想看的时候随手翻开。

这次也把自己的音乐放进来了。代码、文字和音乐，平时看起来是几件不同的事，放在同一个房间里，倒也挺自然。

旧文章还在，新东西慢慢添。先放一张喜欢的唱片，坐一会儿。

## English

I gave my personal website a fresh start today.

I wanted a little studio by the sea at dusk: a pink sky, water and a distant city outside the window, with a CD player on the desk. Each disc opens a project I've worked on. The writing sits nearby as a small stack of newspapers, ready to pick up and read.

My music has a place here too. Code, writing and music usually live in different places, but they feel quite at home in the same room.

The old posts are still here. I'll add new things along the way. For now, put on a favourite record and stay a while.

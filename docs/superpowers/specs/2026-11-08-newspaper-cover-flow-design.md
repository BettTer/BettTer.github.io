# Newspaper Cover Flow

Approved direction: the user selected the proposed light Cover Flow and requested a local preview.

Replace the vertical journal list with a horizontal overlapping newspaper collection. The newest real article starts centered, side papers lean inward. Hover or keyboard focus raises a paper and keeps it featured without recentering the track. Clicking any visible paper opens the existing reader. Horizontal trackpad scrolling, mouse dragging, arrows and keyboard navigation browse the finite collection; normal vertical scrolling remains native. Touch uses native horizontal scrolling and a tap to read.

Approaches: flat overlapping papers feel casual but are harder to browse with many articles; light Cover Flow gives the selected issue a readable front and exposes neighbouring issues. The user chose the latter. No duplicated filler articles or infinite wrap.

Keep cream paper, printed mastheads, fold lines and soft pink window light. Move the coffee cup to a small desk footer. Support all four interface languages, narrow screens, reduced motion, focus return and drag without accidental reading. Preserve article content, original URLs and the existing reader. No carousel dependency. This change is reviewed locally before publishing.
